About the App

This is a simple, minimalist expense tracker to log your income and spending. You can add transactions, view monthly balance, browse your transaction history and check spending insights easily.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?
All your transaction data is saved locally on your device. We do not upload or collect your financial information to any server.

Can I export my data?
Data export feature is coming soon in future updates.

How to delete a transaction?
Swipe left on the transaction entry in the Transactions page to delete or edit it.
